About Us

Your Trusted Handyman

With years of experience and a commitment to quality, we are the go-to team for all your home needs.

D15 Handyman Services is run by Denis Sweeney, a qualified fitter with over 12 years in construction and 13 years as owner of Ultra Cleaning Services. We bring a wealth of experience to every job.

We specialise in pressure washing, home improvements, and flatpack furniture assembly. No job is too big or small – we treat every project with the same care and professionalism.

Based in Clonee, Dublin 15, we serve all of Dublin, Meath, Kildare, Wicklow, and beyond. We're fully insured, affordable, and committed to customer satisfaction.
